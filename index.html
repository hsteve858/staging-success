Performance & Text

  • THE 2076 · Special Topics: Broadway

    The History of the American Musical

    A sample 15-week open curriculum adapted from Backstage Pass: A Survey of American Musical Theatre by Pamyla Stiehl & Bud Coleman. Explores the evolution of American musical theatre across six historical eras, foundational concepts such as the "Golden Triangle" and "Bubble Formula," and the representation of marginalized communities, with modular activities, videos, performance assignments, and analytical rubrics.

    View course materials →
  • THE 2015 · Playwriting

    Playwriting

    A fifteen-module course guiding students through the fundamentals of playwriting, character development, plot structure, dialogue, and thematic exploration, with collaborative discussions and writing tasks inspired by notable plays and theoretical texts.

    View course materials →
  • THE 1008

    Theatre Script Analysis (Part I)

    Reading, interpreting, and evaluating plays through cultural, social, and historical perspectives. Interactive modules, videos, and guided exercises, including character analysis, plot mapping, and page-to-stage simulations, build analytical and production skills, supported by rubrics and glossaries.

    View course materials →
  • THE 1008

    Theatre Script Analysis (Part II)

    Introduces theatre history, script analysis, dramatic structure, visual storytelling, and major theatrical movements, using interactive Sways and original content to build the tools needed to understand and analyze dramatic texts and theatrical works.

    View course materials →
  • THE 1011

    Acting I (Part I)

    Introduces beginning actors to the fundamentals of performance for stage and screen through hands-on practice in scene study, improvisation, and script analysis, guiding students to define acting for themselves and build authentic, emotionally connected characters.

    View course materials →
  • THE 1011

    Acting I (Part II)

    A comprehensive foundation covering scene study, improvisation, and script analysis, with in-depth introductions to Stanislavski, Adler, Meisner, and Hagen, plus tools for character analysis, monologue selection, and mastering dialects and accents.

    View course materials →

Dance

  • DAN 1005 · 1031 · 1043 · 2011 · 2024

    Dance Foundations: An OER Dance Course Collection

    A collection of open curricula for five introductory dance courses — Ballet I, Tap I, Hip Hop I, Dance Composition & Improv, and Dance for Musical Theatre. Each chapter provides a full 15-week schedule with weekly topics, learning objectives, and curated resources, many delivered through interactive, publicly accessible Microsoft Sways.

    View course materials →
  • DAN 1025

    Dance Appreciation

    Covers dance as both a cultural and artistic form, introducing the fundamental elements of dance — body, energy, space, time, and action — across genres from ballet and modern to jazz, hip hop, and world dance, with scholarly readings, multimedia, group projects, and reflective critiques.

    View course materials →

Design & Technical

  • THE 1016 · Technical Theatre

    Technical Theatre

    Covers shop safety, scenic construction, electrics, lighting and sound design, and costume implementation, with multimedia content, curated external resources, and interactive assignments for practical, real-world application.

    View course materials →
  • THE 2000

    Paint / Draw / Render / Model

    Introduces essential techniques in painting, drawing, rendering, and model construction for stage, costume, and lighting design — drafting standards, orthographic projection, perspective drawing, and scale model building — applied to projects rooted in script analysis and design development.

    View course materials →
  • THE 2021

    Set Design for Film & Theatre

    A course companion for students learning scenic design for film and theatre, covering research, drafting, color theory, model construction, visual communication, script analysis, and collaboration, preparing students to create ground plans, painted elevations, and three-dimensional set models.

Costume & Craft

    Basic Costume/Apparel Const

    Free, hands-on materials for mastering costume and apparel basics, hand sewing, textile identification, pattern reading, and sewing-machine operation, alongside the historical evolution of clothing, with interactive modules, videos, and guided projects.

  • THE 2002

    Intermediate Costume Construction

    Combines advanced sewing techniques, garment fitting, heirloom and decorative sewing, and historical context with hands-on projects like skirts, trousers, vests, and jackets, plus an instructor's guide with teaching tips and lesson plans.

  • THE 1009 · Basics of Pattern Drafting

    Basics of Pattern Drafting

    Supports introductory pattern-drafting courses, including a sample adapted syllabus covering measurements, patterns and symbols, drafting bodices, skirts and sleeves, flat pattern manipulation, and grading. Designed for on-campus, synchronous, asynchronous, or blended formats.

    View course materials →
  • THE 1035 · Stage Makeup I

    Stage Makeup I

    Historical context, step-by-step application guides, design planning activities, multimedia recommendations, and practical exercises, with an emphasis on inclusive and culturally responsible practices in character development and storytelling.

  • THE 2022 · Millinery & Costume Accessory

    Millinery & Costume Accessory

    Introduces the craft of costume accessories with an emphasis on millinery, hat and headpiece construction, and related fabrication techniques, including leatherworking, jewelry making, masks, armor, footwear, and specialty costume pieces.

Voice & Career

  • THE 2040 · Theatre Voice & Diction

    Theatre Voice & Diction

    Covers diaphragmatic breathing, structured vocal and physical warm-up sequences, vocal anatomy, factors of sound, and the identification and correction of ten distinct vocal qualities, integrating the International Phonetic Alphabet to master dialect work and standardize theatrical diction.

    View course materials →
  • THE 1029 · Intro-Entertainment Industry

    Intro-Entertainment Industry

    Guides students through the business side of a career in theatre and entertainment, soft skills, trade organizations, resumes, interviewing, and personal branding, building toward a personalized five-year career plan, with worksheets and full slide decks for every session.

    View course materials →
  • THE 1026 · Auditioning for Musical Theatre

    Auditioning for Musical Theatre

    Explores audition psychology, song selection, working with a pianist, résumé standards, and repertoire building, with interactive worksheets, breathing techniques, and performance strategies to build confidence and professionalism.

    View course materials →
  • THE 1052 · Production Stage Management I

    Production Stage Management I

    A practical introduction to the key responsibilities of a stage manager, auditions, rehearsal preparation, paperwork, technical rehearsals, and professional development, with customizable templates, video tutorials, and sample documents including contact sheets and rehearsal reports.

    View course materials →
